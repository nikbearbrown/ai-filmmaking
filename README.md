## Executive summary

- **This folder is now the *AI Filmmaking* course repo (2026-11-17).** The course is for trained filmmakers like Galen, who already have a script and a vision and are new to AI; the AI tools help them get as close to that vision as possible. The plan is in [BLUEPRINT.md](BLUEPRINT.md), with outline.md, vision.md, architecture.md, chapters-spec.md and risks.md beside it (GATE 0 unsigned). The general-audience course, *Agentic Filmmaking*, is a separate repo: [nikbearbrown/agentic-filmmaking-book](https://github.com/nikbearbrown/agentic-filmmaking-book). The skills catalog for both is [agentic-filmmaking-book/skills-catalog/README.md](https://github.com/nikbearbrown/agentic-filmmaking-book/blob/main/skills-catalog/README.md).
- **The question:** can an indie filmmaker with no Hollywood budget now make the film they have carried in their head for years, using AI tools and agents? The YouTube playlist is called **Agentic Filmmaking** (on @NikBearBrown).
- **The test case:** Galen, a filmmaker Bear has known for a long time. He has had a feature in his head for a decade or more and never had the money. His opening scene, in his own words: "Storm at sea with an old Greek ship with black sails arriving into an ancient Athenian port. An old man jumps from the cliff once spotting the black sails. That's the opening of my feature film." He is writing it in script form.
- **Film 1 (built):** a 25-shot walk through a Greek trireme with black sails, made to learn whether an ancient ship can look filmed on location. 249 s, photoreal stills, Kling 4.0 clips, ambient sound, no narration yet.
- **What is in this folder:** this README, `LOCAL-TOOLS.md` (what is installed on Bear's Mac and how to call it), `RULES.md` (draft rules from film 1, to grow with each film), `tools/filmkit.py`, and since 2026-11-17 the course plan files named above.
- **Project files for film 1:** `books/galen/greek-ship` (local, not on GitHub) (script, stills, clips, film). Nothing was moved here.
- **Status 2026-11-06:** playlist and first upload drafted in `books/galen/greek-ship/film/youtube-post.json` (local, not on GitHub), not posted yet. Clips are being upscaled to 4K with Topaz. Liam narration comes next.

## The series and the course (Bear, 2026-11-07)

- **The idea:** Galen is a filmmaker who has spent most of his time raising money to make his film. With AI tools and agents he can now *make* the film, use the footage to raise money more effectively, and submit the finished film to film festivals.
- **The series:** Agentic Filmmaking on YouTube, and an **Agentic Filmmaking course**, both built on Galen's real film as the running example. (2026-11-17: the course for filmmakers like Galen is now *AI Filmmaking*, this repo. *Agentic Filmmaking* is a separate course for a general audience.) Every step (design, storyboard, animate, sound, assemble) is taught from the real work, honest about cost and what failed.
- **Galen's source text:** `books/galen/script` (local, not on GitHub) (his email, verbatim) and SCRIPT-NOTES.md.
- **Storyboard of the whole opening:** `books/galen/opening-storyboard` (local, not on GitHub) (shot list and frames).

## Tools

- Four film skills (2026-11-17, untested on a real shot): [cinematic-shots](https://github.com/nikbearbrown/agentic-filmmaking/blob/main/skills/cinematic-shots/SKILL.md), [film-sound](https://github.com/nikbearbrown/agentic-filmmaking/blob/main/skills/film-sound/SKILL.md), [film-edit](https://github.com/nikbearbrown/agentic-filmmaking/blob/main/skills/film-edit/SKILL.md), [motion-graphics](https://github.com/nikbearbrown/agentic-filmmaking/blob/main/skills/motion-graphics/SKILL.md). They are in the *Agentic Filmmaking* skill repo, [nikbearbrown/agentic-filmmaking](https://github.com/nikbearbrown/agentic-filmmaking), with a guide skill. The research catalog behind them is in [agentic-filmmaking-book/skills-catalog/README.md](https://github.com/nikbearbrown/agentic-filmmaking-book/blob/main/skills-catalog/README.md).
- `tools/filmkit.py`: builds a narrated teaching film from stills, clips and cards (Liam voice, ducked sound, leveled mix, captions). Used for the Aegeus film and the smaller-boat film.
